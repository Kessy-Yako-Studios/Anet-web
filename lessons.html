ADULT PIANO

Have you ever wished you had learned the piano as a child? Or maybe you want to pick it up again?

Read more +

Many adults carry that dream with them for years, often setting it aside as life becomes busy with work, family, and daily responsibilities. The good news is that it is never too late to start.

Whether you’d love to play your favourite songs, unwind at the piano after a long day or between work meetings, or share music with your loved ones, your lessons with me will be tailored entirely around your goals.

My personalised tuition will keep you inspired while helping you make real, meaningful progress. I am sure we will have a lot of fun and some good laughs too.

For adult students, I offer lunch time and evening lesson slots so you can easily fit piano around your schedule and prioritise something just for yourself.

You don’t need any previous experience, just the desire to give it a go.

MUSIC THEORY

Gain a deeper understanding of music and its notation.

Read more +

I currently offer Music Theory lessons based on the ABRSM syllabus up to Grade 5.

These lessons are designed to:

  • • Support your practical music studies by developing a strong understanding of notation, rhythm, harmony, and musical structure
  • • Help you gain a deeper understanding of music and how it is written, structured and performed
  • • Enhance your sight-reading, musicianship, composing, performing, and improvisation skills
  • • Prepare you for the Grade 5 Music Theory exam, which is a prerequisite for ABRSM Practical Grades 6–8

Music theory lessons make everything you play at the piano clearer, more meaningful, and more confident.
