About me

Hello, I'm Aneta, a piano teacher based in London.

With over 15 years of teaching and performing experience, both in the UK and abroad, I have worked with children and adults of all levels, from complete beginners to advanced students, including families and students with additional needs.

Each student I have taught has shaped me as a teacher, and I believe this breadth of experience allows me to adapt my teaching style to the needs of every learner.

Although I have always loved performing, what truly fulfils me is guiding students as they learn and grow, from their very first notes to mastering their favourite pieces. For me, piano lessons are not only about technique or working towards exams, but about joy, expression and developing a lifelong connection with music.

Born in the Czech Republic, I began my musical education at a young age with playing the recorder, a solo and choir singing and composing songs for my girls' music group. All of this eventually led me to discover the piano – an instrument that became very special in my life.

My dedication to music led me to study at the Prague Conservatoire, where I completed a six-year professional programme, followed by Music Pedagogy at Charles University.

Alongside my studies and taking part in concerts and competitions, I began teaching and discovered that sharing music with others was just as meaningful to me as performing.

Watching my students grow in their confidence and their love for music, watching them smile as they learn new skills and pieces, seeing them take pride in their achievements, is what makes teaching so rewarding for me.
