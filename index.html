Welcome to Piano with Aneta

PIANO and MUSIC THEORY lessons in London and Online

If you love the sound of the piano (the most magical instrument in my opinion), you're in the right place. There’s something truly special about what can be created with just a few piano keys, and I'd love for you to experience it.

I teach piano lessons to both adults and children, to complete beginners and to those returning after some time away.

You can start learning the piano at any time, and with the right guidance and a bit of practice you can begin playing the music you love in a very short time! With the knowledge, skills and passion I bring, I'll support you to reach your musical dream.

Bring the joy of music into your life today 🖤
