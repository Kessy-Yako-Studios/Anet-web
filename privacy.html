Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20.6.2026

Who I am

I am Aneta Mertova, a self-employed piano teacher operating as a sole trader in the United Kingdom.

What information I collect

When you contact me through this website, I may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • The information you include in your message

How I use your information

I use this information only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Provide information about piano and music theory lessons
  • Arrange and manage lessons where applicable

My lawful basis for processing this information is my legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and providing piano tuition services.

How your information is stored

Contact form submissions are processed through Formspree and delivered to my email inbox.

I take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including securing my email account with two-factor authentication.

I do not sell or share your personal information for marketing purposes.

How long I keep your information

I keep personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Deal with your enquiry
  • Provide services where lessons are booked

If no ongoing service is established, enquiries are normally deleted or archived within a reasonable period.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise these rights, please contact me at:
Pianowithaneta@gmail.com

I may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

I will respond to your request within one month in accordance with UK GDPR.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how your personal data is handled, you can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO):
https://ico.org.uk

Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
